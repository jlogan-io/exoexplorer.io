What ExoExplorer is

ExoExplorer is an educational visualisation of public astronomical data. It is not a scientific instrument, and it is not a substitute for the archive or the published research behind it.

About the data

Planetary and stellar values come from the NASA Exoplanet Archive and can contain errors, omissions and later revisions. Some values are calculated by the app, and those are marked in amber. Scenes are scaled and styled to be readable: Log scale compresses distances, small planets are drawn larger than true scale, faint stars are drawn brighter than they appear, and the nebula is decorative.

Don’t rely on ExoExplorer for research or any purpose where accuracy is critical. Check the NASA Exoplanet Archive and the original publications instead.

Generated summaries

Some summaries are written by an on-device language model. They are written only from archive values and checked against them automatically, but they can still be wrong or incomplete. Each summary says how it was written, so you can tell a generated one from a hand-written one.
